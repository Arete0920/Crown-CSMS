# Crown Solomon Canon

## Status
Official canon.

## Product Classification
Crown Solomon is a Crown knowledge, guidance, onboarding, governance-template, interpretation, and success-enablement platform.

It supports Crown adoption, responsible use, and mission-aware implementation across the Crown ecosystem.

## Purpose
Crown Solomon exists to reduce implementation friction, increase adoption quality, strengthen leadership confidence, and provide mission-aware guidance for schools using Crown.

## What Crown Solomon Is
Crown Solomon includes:
- structured onboarding guidance
- mission-aware implementation support
- contextual knowledge resources
- governance templates
- policy setup guidance
- decision-support interpretation guidance
- intervention playbooks
- best-practice operating guidance
- board education resources
- administrator enablement content

## What Crown Solomon Is Not
Crown Solomon is not:
- the predictive analytics engine
- the scenario model
- the system of record
- a daily transaction system
- a substitute for school leadership

## Relationship to Crown Compass
Solomon supports Compass by helping schools:
- define mission-aligned parameters
- configure governance thresholds
- interpret forecasts and risk indicators
- understand board-facing outputs
- document policies and assumptions
- choose responsible follow-up actions

## Relationship to Crown Discernment
Solomon does not generate predictive outputs.

Solomon supports Discernment use by providing:
- explanatory language
- intervention playbooks
- recommended review processes
- governance templates
- role-specific training
- documentation patterns
- “what this means / what this does not mean” guidance

## Canonical Product Job
Compass is the leadership strategy surface.  
Discernment is the explainable engine inside Compass.  
Solomon is the human-guidance layer around both.

## Experience Design Rules
Solomon content should appear as:
- contextual guidance
- implementation support
- interpretation notes
- “next step” resources
- governance and policy templates

Solomon should not be presented as:
- a chatbot oracle
- an autonomous recommender
- a source of hidden algorithmic judgment

## Commercial Position
Solomon is a strategic adoption, support, and differentiation asset.

It strengthens:
- onboarding quality
- implementation confidence
- client success
- retention
- mission alignment
- switching-cost durability

## Canonical External Positioning
Crown Solomon is Crown’s mission-aware implementation and guidance platform, helping Christian schools configure, understand, and use Crown responsibly and effectively.
