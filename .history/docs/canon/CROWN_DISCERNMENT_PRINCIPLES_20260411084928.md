# Crown Discernment Principles

## Status
Official principles canon.

## Definition
Crown Discernment is the explainable predictive analytics and scenario-modeling engine that powers selected Crown Compass capabilities.

Discernment is a decision-support instrument.  
It is not a decision-maker.

## Core Principle
Discernment informs leadership judgment; it does not replace it.

## Non-Negotiable Principles

### 1. Mission Before Optimization
Discernment must operate within mission-defined, leadership-defined, and board-reviewable parameters.

The system may optimize within approved constraints.  
It may not redefine those constraints on its own.

### 2. Human Judgment Above Algorithmic Output
No Discernment output may be treated as final authority for:
- admissions denial
- discipline action
- financial aid denial
- pastoral judgment
- spiritual judgment
- student labeling
- employment judgment
- family treatment
without explicit human review and institution-owned policy governance.

### 3. Explainability Required
All predictive and scenario outputs must be explainable in practical human terms.

Outputs must show:
- what was evaluated
- what assumptions were used
- what factors most influenced the result
- what limits or uncertainty exist

### 4. Auditability Required
Every material model run or scenario output must be auditable.

The platform should preserve, when implemented:
- model version
- data snapshot date
- feature set version
- threshold version
- policy parameter set
- output payload
- explanation payload
- generation timestamp

### 5. Persons Are Not Scores
No person may be reduced to a score, label, or machine-defined identity.

Language must avoid:
- “problem student”
- “flight-risk family”
- “bad-fit profile”
- “spiritual score”
- “character score”
- “moral score”

Approved language:
- indicator
- signal
- pattern
- support flag
- intervention cue
- scenario output

### 6. Restorative Over Punitive Use
When Discernment surfaces risk or concern, default response should prioritize:
- support
- review
- outreach
- intervention
- stewardship
- care
rather than automatic punishment, exclusion, or stigma.

### 7. Governance-Defined Parameters
Institutions must control their own approved thresholds, assumptions, and policy parameters.

Discernment may not operate as a hidden or self-governing model outside institutional oversight.

### 8. No Spiritual Reductionism
Discernment must not be used to assign spiritual worth, discipleship value, moral rank, or theological standing.

Mission-related signals may support institutional visibility only when framed with humility, governance, and clear non-reductive intent.

### 9. Transparent Communication
All user-facing communication about Discernment must emphasize:
- transparency
- accountability
- support for judgment
- explainability
- mission alignment
- governance control

### 10. Add-on Boundary Preserved
Discernment belongs under Crown Compass in the add-on layer and must not invade Crown Core.

## Canonical Summary Statement
Crown Discernment exists to support wise, accountable, mission-aligned leadership through transparent analytics. It does not replace prayer, pastoral care, leadership judgment, family partnership, or board governance.
