PRODUCT DIRECTION

Designed around responsible scholarship operations.

The following capabilities describe intended product direction, not an available production service.

01

Applications & verification

Planned program-specific intake, renewals, verification workflows, document review, and consent-governed information exchange.

02

Eligibility & awards

Separate statutory eligibility from financial-need analysis and preserve human authorization, program provenance, and an auditable decision trail.

03

School coordination

Planned enrollment and eligible-charge verification, noncash commitments, payment reconciliation, and SGO-approved reporting.

INTENDED WORKFLOW

From application to accountable reconciliation.

This sequence is conceptual. It does not represent an enabled payment or live eligibility determination process.

  1. 01

    Family applies

    The family submits information required by its specific scholarship program.

  2. 02

    SGO verifies

    Authorized grantor personnel confirm program requirements and supporting evidence.

  3. 03

    SGO decides

    Human reviewers determine eligibility and awards under SGO-approved rules.

  4. 04

    School confirms

    Participating schools verify enrollment and qualifying educational charges.

  5. 05

    SGO funds

    Only the SGO and its authorized payment providers control real funds movement.

  6. 06

    Both reconcile

    Commitments, cleared funds, allocations, exceptions, and reporting are reconciled.

CURRENT PRODUCT STATUS

Pilot development. Clear boundaries.

A narrow pilot-only engineering foundation exists for grantor records, membership, versioned programs, pseudonymous applications, eligibility attestations, and noncash commitments.

Not certified for operational use

A standalone portal, complete program administration, real-money settlement, donor and restricted-fund accounting, financial integrations, regulatory reports, and operational certification remain unverified or unfinished.

Jireh SGO is not an IRS-certified scholarship grantor, payment processor, or substitute for legal and tax advice. No production compliance claim is made.
