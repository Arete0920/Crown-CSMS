01 / CROWN JIREH

Financial aid shaped by mission and responsible stewardship.

Arete's Mission-Aligned Strategic Financial Aid Framework informs the intended decision structure. The school controls its own approved policies, eligibility standards, award decisions and exceptions.

01

Mission and community

Consider school mission, community commitments and the role of financial aid in supporting enrollment and belonging.

02

Student contribution

Consider contributions to school life, leadership and programs within a consistent, board-approved policy.

03

Enrollment and capacity

Understand grade-level enrollment, available seats, retention priorities and sustainable net tuition.

04

Access and affordability

Review household circumstances carefully while respecting family dignity, confidentiality and human judgment.

05

Retention and hardship

Provide a structured path for changed circumstances, continuity and annual reconsideration.

06

Governance and accountability

Document recommendation, approval authority, adjustments, exceptions and financial reconciliation.

02 / SCHOOL AID DECISION PATHWAY

From family application to institutional learning.

The intended connected workflow spans application, assessment, recommendation, approval, communication, renewal and reporting. The exact functions available in a specific release require demonstration and written confirmation.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Collect an appropriate application and evidence.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    Assess circumstances and school-approved policy.

  3. 03

    Recommend

    Document purpose, proposed aid and net-tuition implications.

  4. 04

    Approve

    Preserve authorized human decisions and audit history.

  5. 05

    Communicate

    Explain the decision and next steps respectfully.

  6. 06

    Renew and measure

    Manage changes, reconsideration and aggregate outcomes.

School awards, household ledger credits and external scholarships follow distinct financial paths. Actual payment processing remains subject to separate production certification.

03 / RELATED OFFERING

Jireh SGO: scholarship-grantor administration.

Jireh SGO is being developed for scholarship-granting organizations that administer independent grant programs, donor restrictions, family verification, award decisions, participating-school relationships and reporting.

Its planned workflow is: family applies; SGO verifies; SGO decides; school confirms; the authorized SGO funds; both parties reconcile.

Discuss an SGO design partnership →

SGO release status: pilot development

A narrow engineering foundation supports some SGO organization, membership, program, application and noncash commitment models. Standalone production operation, real-money settlement, regulatory compliance and complete program workflows have not been certified.

Jireh SGO is a technology product, not a scholarship-granting organization. Authorized SGOs retain eligibility, award and funding authority. Independent legal and program review is required.
