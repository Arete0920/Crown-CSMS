## Tuition policy

HCA published tuition is grade/program based.

Published tuition includes:

- Standard academic program
- Standard curriculum
- Textbooks
- Standard technology
- Standard academic resources
- Routine grade-level academic operating costs

These may be tracked internally as tuition components, but they are not separate parent-facing routine fees.

## Tuition reduction policy

All tuition reductions go through Financial Aid.

HCA does not use:

- Sibling tuition discounts
- Staff tuition discounts
- Church/member tuition discounts
- Hardship discounts outside Financial Aid
- Bulk/family tuition discounts

## Application and re-enrollment fee

HCA application/re-enrollment fee rule:

- First child in family cycle: 85.00
- Each additional child in same family cycle: 50.00

This fee is:

- Standalone
- Due upfront
- Not tuition
- Not rolled into a payment plan
- Not eligible as a tuition reduction

## Financial Aid application fee

HCA Financial Aid application/admin fee:

- Amount: 40.00
- Billing level: family
- Frequency: once per aid application cycle
- Due upfront when applying for Financial Aid

This fee is:

- Not per child
- Not tuition
- Not rolled into a payment plan
- Not discounted for multiple children

## Enrollment deposit

HCA does not use a separate standard enrollment deposit in this sprint.

Once accepted and Financial Aid is finalized or marked not applicable:

- Parent selects a payment plan.
- First tuition payment is created from the selected payment plan.

## Payment plan options

HCA payment plans:

1. Annual
2. Twice-a-year
3. Monthly 10
4. Monthly 12

Plan defaults:

- Annual: 1 installment
- Twice-a-year: 2 installments
- Monthly 10: 10 installments
- Monthly 12: 12 installments

Application/re-enrollment fees and Financial Aid application fees are excluded from these payment plans.
