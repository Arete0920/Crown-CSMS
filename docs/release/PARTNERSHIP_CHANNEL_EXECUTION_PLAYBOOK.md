## Referral and Case-Study Flywheel Rules

1. Anchor concessions require explicit case-study and referral deliverables.
2. Every pilot school has a named reference-readiness owner.
3. Conference leads must enter tracked trial workflows within one business day.
4. Reference assets must map to proven product scope and evidence state.

## Channel Governance Rules

1. No partner commitments that bypass Core/Modules/Add-ons contract boundaries.
2. No market claims beyond release evidence state.
3. No cohort launch without Priority #7 implementation/support readiness.
4. No segment expansion unless previous phase metrics are at or above target.

## Required Weekly Dashboard Metrics

1. Partner-sourced opportunities opened
2. Partner-sourced opportunities converted
3. Cohort schools launched and time-to-live
4. Reference schools secured and published
5. Retention and expansion by acquisition channel
