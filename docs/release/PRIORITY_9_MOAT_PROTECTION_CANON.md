## Purpose

Define how Crown protects and compounds durable enterprise value after market-capture begins.
Priority #9 governs moat defense, payment-routing discipline, onboarding and success quality, and continuity-capital execution discipline.

## Priority Stack (Authoritative)

1. Core proof and release path
2. First-wave modules
3. Second-wave operational modules

5. Packaging, governance, and pilot-readiness
6. Later add-ons and standalone-capable expansion products

8. Partnership-led market capture and expansion
9. Moat protection, retention discipline, payment-routing enforcement, and enterprise-value compounding

## Non-Negotiable #1: Protect the Dual Moat

Crown defensibility is governed as a combined moat:

1. Mission-native architecture and operating model
2. CompuWerx-linked payment rail economics
3. Reference-customer and association network reputation effects

The moat is measured by retention durability, partner expansion depth, and payment-rail adherence.

## Non-Negotiable #2: Enforce Payment-Routing Discipline

Payment-routing integrity is treated as enterprise-critical revenue protection:

1. Contract terms must require approved routing pathways
2. School onboarding must include routing obligations and controls
3. Product and operations must detect off-platform routing leakage
4. Escalation and remediation playbooks must trigger on leakage indicators
5. Commercial exceptions require executive approval and documented risk acceptance

## Non-Negotiable #3: Make Onboarding and Customer Success a Moat

Customer success is a moat function, not a support afterthought:

1. Crown Solomon-aligned implementation quality standards
2. Structured onboarding with adoption and training milestones
3. Dedicated customer-success ownership for reference readiness
4. Proactive health interventions before churn risk hardens
5. Conference and peer reputation protection through delivery quality

## Non-Negotiable #4: Preserve Team Continuity and Capital Discipline

Scaling must preserve execution continuity and disciplined capital use:

1. Continuity of critical leadership and delivery ownership
2. Capital deployment sequence aligned to plan priorities
3. No opportunistic expansion that weakens operating discipline
4. Hiring and budget decisions tied to measurable moat outcomes
5. Governance review cadence for continuity and burn efficiency

## Required Moat Metrics and Guardrails

1. Payment routing compliance rate
2. Net revenue retention and expansion by cohort
3. Time-to-adoption and onboarding completion quality
4. Partner reference-school conversion and durability
5. Team continuity and key-role stability metrics
