# Priority #8 Partnership-Led Market Capture Canon

Status: Market expansion governance policy
Updated: 2026-04-14

## Purpose

Define the post-operations growth layer where Crown transitions from readiness to market-share execution.
Priority #8 governs partnership channels, referral flywheels, ecosystem expansion, and deliberate TAM expansion.

## Priority Stack (Authoritative)

1. Core proof and release path
2. First-wave modules
3. Second-wave operational modules
4. First-wave add-ons and differentiators
5. Packaging, governance, and pilot-readiness
6. Later add-ons and standalone-capable expansion products
7. Implementation, support, integrations, and scale operations
8. Partnership-led market capture and expansion

## Governing Rule

Priority #8 is channel-first and scale-first.
No market expansion plan may bypass Core truth, Module reliability, Add-on contract boundaries, or Priority #7 operating discipline.

## 8A. Association and Network Channel Lock (First)

Execute high-leverage partner channels before one-school-at-a-time prospecting:

1. ACSI regional agreements
2. Denomination network agreements
3. Microsoft Education partner referrals
4. Channel cohorts sized for 20-50 school wins

## 8B. Referral and Case-Study Flywheel (Second)

Convert anchor-school proof into compounding demand:

1. Anchor-school concessions tied to evidence and references
2. Co-marketing rights with defined publication criteria
3. Administrator referral pipeline model
4. Conference-to-trial-to-contract conversion pathway
5. Case-study production cadence per cohort

## 8C. Broader Association Ecosystem Expansion (Third)

Scale from early channel wins into formal network licensing:

1. Association licensing model and pricing policy
2. Channel development playbook for major school networks
3. Partner enablement package and onboarding path
4. Governance for multi-school rollout velocity and quality

## 8D. Deliberate Addressable-Market Expansion (Fourth)

Expand outside the core channel only after Christian-school channel repeatability is proven:

1. Secular and daycare expansion sequence
2. Referral program extension strategy
3. Adjacent acquisition channels with stage gates
4. Segment-specific messaging without scope drift

## Operating Spine for Priority #8

1. Platform lead (TC): institutional partner relationships, partner strategy, GTM oversight
2. Marketing director: partnership execution, conference channel, referral and positioning programs
3. Client success manager: onboarding quality, reference development, pilot retention and expansion

## Required Metrics and Gates

1. Channel-sourced pipeline share
2. Cohort conversion rate (intro to contract)
3. Time-to-live for partner-sourced schools
4. Reference-school production rate
5. Retention and expansion rate by channel source
