**Status:** Canonical engineering governance

## Development assistance

Commercial development tools and automated services supported portions of implementation, debugging, testing, analysis, documentation, review, and repository operations. They are not product owners, human contributors, architecture authorities, independent human reviewers, acceptance authorities, or release authorities. CROWN does not depend on such a service at runtime.

Active product, buyer, and owner-facing materials use this vendor-neutral description. Product or vendor names belong only in security, licensing, contractual, or historical evidence where specifically material.

## Accountability and attribution

- Human owners remain accountable for scope, correctness, verification, security, and acceptance.
- Automated checks are technical evidence, not independent human approval.
- Precise contribution claims require the Contribution Evidence Ledger and durable evidence.
- Do not rewrite history, infer authorship from style, manufacture attribution, or convert `NOT VERIFIED` into a definitive external claim.
- The repository owner cannot independently approve their own work where independent review is required.

## Pull-request disclosure

Non-trivial pull requests record the human owner, evidence-backed contributors, implementation support, human verification actually performed, and unresolved attribution. Keep the disclosure factual and concise.
