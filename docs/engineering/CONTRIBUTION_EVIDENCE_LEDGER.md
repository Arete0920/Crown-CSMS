## Evidence statuses

- `VERIFIED`: direct durable evidence supports the claim.
- `FOUNDER ATTESTED`: the Founder/Product Owner attested to the stated boundary.
- `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`: evidence supports only the recorded portion.
- `NOT VERIFIED`: evidence is missing or insufficient.
- `DISPUTED`: sources conflict; do not use externally.
- `SUPERSEDED`: a newer record replaces the claim.

## Attribution rules

Do not manufacture attribution, rewrite historical Git authorship for presentation, treat invitation or access metadata as contribution proof, or describe automated verification as independent human review. Preserve genuine commit, pull-request, issue, workflow, and release history as technical provenance.

Current owner-facing documentation should present one accurate development narrative: years of founder research, planning, domain expertise, and workflow design; approximately seven months of intensive solo software development; extensive testing, hardening, certification, and release preparation; and responsible transfer of CROWN to successor ownership.
