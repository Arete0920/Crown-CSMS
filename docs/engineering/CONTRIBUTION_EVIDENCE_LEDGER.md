## Evidence statuses

- `VERIFIED`: direct durable evidence supports the claim.
- `FOUNDER ATTESTED`: the Founder/Product Owner attested to the stated boundary.
- `PARTIALLY VERIFIED`: evidence supports only the recorded portion.
- `NOT VERIFIED`: evidence is missing or insufficient.
- `DISPUTED`: sources conflict; do not use externally.
- `SUPERSEDED`: a newer record replaces the claim.

Do not manufacture attribution, alter historical authorship for presentation, treat submission metadata as complete authorship proof, or describe automated verification as independent human review.
