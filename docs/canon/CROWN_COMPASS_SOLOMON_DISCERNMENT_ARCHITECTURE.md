# Crown Compass / Solomon / Discernment Architecture

## Official Structure

### Crown Compass
Leadership, board, institutional health, mission-alignment, scenario, and strategic insight platform.

### Discernment Engine
Explainable predictive analytics and scenario-modeling engine inside Crown Compass.

### Crown Solomon
Mission-aware knowledge, interpretation, onboarding, implementation, governance-template, and action-guidance layer supporting both.

## Canonical Formula
Crown Compass = leadership-facing strategic product
Discernment Engine = predictive and explanatory engine inside Compass
Crown Solomon = guidance and implementation support around Compass and Discernment

## Safe Product Sentence
Crown Compass, powered by the Discernment Engine and supported by Crown Solomon.

## Layer Placement
- Crown Core: system-of-record truth
- Crown Modules: operational workflows
- Crown Add-ons: strategic and optional products
- Crown Compass: add-on
- Discernment Engine: inside Compass add-on
- Crown Solomon: support/guidance layer for adoption and use

## Data Boundary
Core owns truth.
Modules extend truth through defined workflows.
Compass consumes governed truth.
Discernment analyzes governed truth.
Solomon guides interpretation and action.

## UX Boundary
Core and modules handle daily operations.
Compass handles leadership intelligence and governance-facing visibility.
Solomon handles guidance, templates, interpretation, and playbooks.

## Messaging Boundary
Compass is the visible strategic product.
Discernment is the intelligence engine.
Solomon is the guidance platform.

Do not flatten all three into one indistinct message.

## Safe Build Sequence
1. Approve canons
2. Approve product taxonomy alignment
3. Approve data contracts
4. Document v1 Discernment use case
5. Keep implementation conceptual until governance, UX, and data boundaries are reviewed
6. Integrate later through explicit add-on contracts
