# Crown Compass Canon

## Status
Official canon.

## Product Classification
Crown Compass is a **Crown Add-on** in the **Leadership & Strategy** category.

It is **not** part of Crown Core.
It is **not** a first-wave operational module.
It must integrate with Crown through approved APIs, services, events, and permission-scoped data access only.

## Purpose
Crown Compass is Crown’s executive, board, governance, institutional health, and mission-alignment platform.

It exists to help school leadership and boards:
- evaluate institutional health
- view mission-aligned strategic indicators
- review board-ready reporting
- examine scenario outcomes within mission-defined and governance-defined constraints
- support accountable, transparent stewardship

## What Crown Compass Is
Crown Compass is the leadership-facing strategic intelligence layer of Crown.

It includes:
- institutional health views
- governance reporting
- mission-alignment reporting
- board-ready summaries and exports
- leadership scenario analysis
- trend analysis
- policy-parameter-based forecasting
- explainable predictive outputs powered by the Discernment Engine

## What Crown Compass Is Not
Crown Compass is not:
- Crown Core
- the system of record
- the admissions system
- the billing system
- an autonomous AI decision-maker
- a black-box analytics product
- a replacement for prayer, leadership judgment, pastoral care, family partnership, or board oversight

## Relationship to Crown Discernment
Crown Discernment is the predictive analytics and scenario engine **inside Crown Compass**.

Crown Compass is the visible leadership product.
Discernment is the explanatory and forecasting engine that powers it.

Approved language:
- “Crown Compass, powered by the Discernment Engine”
- “Discernment-powered institutional forecasting inside Crown Compass”

Disallowed positioning:
- Discernment as a separate flagship product
- Discernment as a standalone Crown Core capability
- Discernment as a replacement for leadership judgment

## Relationship to Crown Solomon
Crown Solomon is the mission-aware knowledge, implementation, governance-template, interpretation, and action-guidance layer that supports Crown Compass.

Compass shows leadership insight.
Discernment explains projections and patterns.
Solomon helps leaders configure, interpret, and act wisely.

## Data and Boundary Rules
Crown Compass may consume data from Crown Core and approved modules through defined contracts only.

Compass may read:
- enrollment trends
- attendance summaries
- billing aggregates
- retention indicators
- mission metrics
- governance settings
- defined institutional thresholds
- approved strategic KPI data

Compass must not become the canonical owner of:
- student records
- household records
- transcript truth
- attendance truth
- billing truth
- operational workflow state

Core owns truth.
Compass consumes governed truth.

## UI Position
Compass UI is an add-on interface for leadership, board, executive, and strategic users.

It should remain separate from:
- daily operator dashboards
- teacher workflow screens
- registrar transaction screens
- finance operations screens

## Pricing and Packaging Position
Compass belongs in the mission / leadership / strategy add-on family.

It may be sold:
- as part of a Mission Suite
- as part of a leadership/governance package
- as a premium strategic add-on

It must not be assumed to be included in the base Crown Core package by default.

## Governance Requirements
Every Compass output that includes forecasting, risk, or scenario analysis must be:
- transparent
- auditable
- parameter-bound
- version-aware
- human-reviewed
- suitable for board-level explanation

## Canonical External Positioning
Crown Compass is Crown’s mission-aligned governance and institutional strategy platform, powered by transparent predictive intelligence and designed for accountable Christian school leadership.
