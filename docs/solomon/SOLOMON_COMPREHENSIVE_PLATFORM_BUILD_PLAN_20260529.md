# SOLOMON Comprehensive Platform Build Plan

Date: 2026-05-29
Status: Strategic Direction (Governance-Led)
Scope: CROWN ecosystem and future standalone platform packaging

## Executive Vision

SOLOMON is the governed institutional intelligence layer for CROWN and, over time, a standalone Christian education intelligence platform.

Positioning doctrine:

```text
CROWN executes.
SOLOMON informs.
Microsoft collaborates.
Governance controls.
Decision-support remains subordinate to human governance and review.
```

## Strategic Identity

SOLOMON is:
- institutional memory,
- governed knowledge base,
- curriculum/resource intelligence layer,
- Christian education advisory system,
- contextual guidance engine,
- provenance-aware content registry,
- operational playbook repository,
- spiritual-life resource framework.

SOLOMON is not:
- a chatbot,

Core category language:

```text
Governed Christian Education Intelligence Platform
```

## Foundational Principles

### 1) Governance First

Every knowledge item should remain accountable to owner, approver, source, visibility, review date, lifecycle state, and tenant scope.

### 2) Human Authority

SOLOMON may assist discovery and organization, but must not silently approve, publish, reclassify, or mutate governance-critical fields.

### 3) Quiet Intelligence

Externally use guidance language, not AI branding. Keep AI assistive and internal by default.

### 4) Publisher Respect

Use metadata + official-source references first. No copyrighted bulk ingestion without explicit permission.

### 5) Microsoft Alignment

Microsoft stores and collaborates. SOLOMON governs meaning and context. CROWN executes workflows.

## Publisher Strategy

Initial BJU Press posture:

```text
official-source metadata index only
```

Allowed:
- title,
- grade band,
- subject,
- edition,
- resource type,
- official source URL,
- availability flags,
- license requirement and partnership status.

Not allowed:
- scraping,
- mirroring,
- replication of copyrighted content,
- unauthorized bulk ingestion.

Apply the same rights-first pattern to other publishers.

## Automation Strategy

External posture:
- guided support,
- contextual resource delivery,
- trusted knowledge support,
- role-based recommendations.

Internal AI assist only:
- classification suggestions,
- duplicate detection,
- summarization drafts,
- policy comparison,
- crosswalk suggestions,
- relevance ranking.

Hard AI boundary:
- no autonomous approvals,
- no doctrine or policy authority,
- no lifecycle or visibility mutation.
