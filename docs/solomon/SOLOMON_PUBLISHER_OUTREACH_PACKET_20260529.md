## Purpose

This packet defines the external posture SOLOMON uses when contacting curriculum and resource publishers.

SOLOMON is not requesting bulk content ingestion, redistribution rights, or hidden training access. It is requesting permission to maintain a governed metadata and link index that helps schools discover official resources while preserving publisher ownership.

## Outreach Positioning

Use this language when introducing SOLOMON to a publisher:

> SOLOMON is a governed Christian education intelligence platform. We maintain a rights-safe index of official-source metadata, licensing posture, and resource links so schools can discover your materials without copying or republishing your content.

> We prefer metadata-only collaboration unless a publisher explicitly grants broader permission in writing.

## What We Are Asking For

1. Official resource landing pages, catalog pages, and support pages.
2. Permission to record metadata such as title, grade band, subject, edition, format, and official URL.
3. Any published licensing or partnership terms that apply to school use.
4. A named contact point for partnership or permissions follow-up.
5. Confirmation of whether a resource is canonical, advisory, or reference-only in SOLOMON.

## What We Are Not Asking For

1. No scraping of protected content.
2. No mirroring of curriculum text, assessments, answer keys, or teacher guides.
3. No bulk ingestion of copyrighted materials.
4. No automated republication.
5. No implied training rights.
6. No changes to publisher ownership or distribution controls.

## Rights-Safe Partnership Terms

SOLOMON should offer the following baseline terms:

- official-link indexing only unless expanded permission is granted,
- attribution to the publisher for all official resources,
- clear license-required flags in the source manifest,
- review cadence for keeping links and editions current,
- immediate removal or deactivation of stale or revoked references,
- no autonomous publication of publisher content.

## Recommended First Outreach Targets

Priority sequence:

1. BJU Press
2. Core curriculum publishers already used by the school
3. Resource vendors with explicit school-partnership programs
4. Supplemental support publishers with public catalogs

## Sample Outreach Email

Subject: Rights-Safe Metadata Collaboration for SOLOMON

Hello,

We are building SOLOMON, a governed Christian education intelligence platform used to help schools find and organize official curriculum resources.

We would like to maintain a metadata-only index of your official resources, including title, grade band, subject, edition, and official source URLs. We will not scrape, mirror, or republish copyrighted content, and we will not use your materials outside the scope you authorize in writing.

If you have a partner or permissions contact, we would appreciate the best place to send follow-up questions. We can also share the exact metadata fields we intend to track if helpful.

Thank you,

SOLOMON Governance Team
