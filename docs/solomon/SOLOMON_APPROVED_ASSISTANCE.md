# Solomon approved assistance catalog

Version: solomon-assistance-v1. Maintained by CROWN. Content is repository-owned,
general adult assistance, served without runtime external generation. It is not
independently certified.
The owner authorized this expansion on October 4, 2026. Human review is required
before use; authorization to implement is not a legal clearance or content license
for unrelated materials.

The existing school-scoped endpoint and independent default-off frontend/backend
flags serve these resources. Inputs remain exactly a maintained topic and a human
review acknowledgement. No free text, uploads, user-selected sources, records,
metrics, recipient lists, network transport, or operational writes are accepted.
All nine resources are available from the shared Solomon help dialog when enabled
for authorized staff. Checklists and drafts are plain text with maintained-source
links; no draft is automatically saved, sent, or acted on.

## Existing guidance

Implementation, forecast interpretation, operating governance, and the human
advisory handoff retain their maintained guidance in `backend/solomon/guidance.py`.
The source digest now covers the complete returned resource, including its title,
checklist, draft, source reference, and catalog version.

## knowledge

### Find reliable CROWN answers

Use published page help and maintained CROWN documentation. Confirm the documented version and source before applying instructions. If an answer is absent or conflicts with your configuration, ask authorized support; do not invent an answer.

1. Open Solomon on the relevant page.
2. Review the published instructions and their scope.
3. Verify permissions and configuration with the responsible adult.
4. Escalate missing or conflicting guidance to support.

```text
Support request outline
Module: [module name]
Expected behavior: [general description]
Documentation reviewed: [approved reference]
Question: [general operational question]
Keep records, credentials, screenshots of personal data, and private logs out of this outline.
```

## training

### Prepare adult staff training

Use a synthetic practice environment and a role-specific agenda. Confirm local permissions, provide accessible instructions, and have the implementation owner approve the session before delivery.

1. Select the adult staff audience and learning objectives.
2. Demonstrate the relevant workflow using synthetic examples.
3. Allow supervised practice and questions.
4. Record completion through the school's approved training process.

```text
Staff training agenda
1. Purpose and responsibilities
2. Navigation and permissions
3. Demonstration with synthetic examples
4. Supervised practice
5. Troubleshooting and support
6. Human acceptance review before live use
```

## communications

### Prepare a general announcement

Adapt this generic draft outside Solomon using your authorized communication workflow. Verify dates, audience, links, accessibility, and school approval before sending. Solomon does not select recipients or send messages.

1. Choose the announcement purpose.
2. Replace every placeholder in the approved communication editor.
3. Check facts, audience permissions, and accessible wording.
4. Obtain human approval before sending.

```text
Subject: Reminder about [event]

Our school will hold [event] on [date] at [time] in [location]. Please review [approved school link] for preparation and participation details. For general questions, contact [school office].

Thank you for partnering with our school.

Draft only: verify all details and obtain approval before sending.
```

## teaching

### Prepare a teacher-reviewed lesson outline

Use school-approved curriculum and materials you have permission to use. Teachers select content, biblical alignment, age suitability, and assessment criteria. Do not submit student work, grades, accommodations, or personal learning profiles.

1. Choose a curriculum objective from approved materials.
2. Plan a brief introduction, model, guided practice, and independent practice.
3. Create an assessment rubric aligned to the objective.
4. Review accessibility, accuracy, and school expectations before teaching.

```text
Lesson planning outline
Objective: [approved curriculum objective]
Materials: [rights-cleared resources]
Introduction: [connect to prior learning]
Teacher model: [worked example]
Guided practice: [general activity]
Independent practice: [general task]
Reflection: [discussion question]
Rubric: accuracy, reasoning, clarity, and appropriate use of sources
Teacher review required before use.
```

## leadership

### Prepare a leadership meeting agenda

Use this generic agenda to organize human discussion. Keep actual minutes, personnel matters, confidential deliberations, school finances, and individual cases outside Solomon. Leadership remains accountable for policy and decisions.

1. Confirm meeting purpose and accountable leader.
2. Identify policies requiring human discussion and approval.
3. Prepare authorized materials through the school's normal process.
4. Assign owners and record decisions in the approved governance system.

```text
Leadership agenda
1. Opening reflection and purpose
2. Review previous approved action items
3. Mission and operational priorities
4. Policy review questions
5. Staff training and implementation needs
6. Decisions reserved for authorized leaders
7. Owners, deadlines, and next meeting
```

## outreach

### Prepare general Kingdom Path outreach

Use verified public sources and authorized school messaging. Check publication dates, geographic scope, and reuse rights. Do not infer family circumstances, rank prospects, or target identifiable people. School-specific tuition and enrollment strategy requires human advisory support.

1. Define a general public audience and outreach objective.
2. Verify public source dates, definitions, and reuse rights.
3. Have school leadership approve every factual claim.
4. Publish only through the school's authorized channels.

```text
Public outreach outline
Who we serve: [approved general description]
Our mission: [approved mission statement]
Learning opportunities: [verified program descriptions]
Invitation: [approved public event or inquiry link]
Contact: [public school office contact]
Do not insert invented enrollment, outcome, scholarship, or demographic claims.
```

## care

### Plan Diadem, care, camp, and activity operations

Use a general planning checklist with authorized adult staff. Actual supervision, attendance, health, emergency, and pickup decisions must follow approved school procedures and qualified human direction. This checklist is not emergency or licensing advice.

1. Confirm program purpose, approved hours, and responsible adult leaders.
2. Review applicable licensing and school procedures with qualified staff.
3. Plan staff training, supplies, accessible activities, and communication.
4. Use established systems for attendance, health, safeguarding, and authorized pickup.

```text
Program planning outline
Purpose and approved schedule
Accountable staff and training
Facilities, supplies, and accessible activities
Approved family communication process
Required human review of supervision and emergency procedures
Acceptance checklist before opening
```

## accessibility

### Prepare clear, accessible general instructions

Use short sentences, descriptive links, clear headings, and one action per step. Have an appropriate reviewer check translations and safety or policy wording. This resource does not certify accessibility compliance or provide automatic translation.

1. State the purpose in familiar language.
2. Use one action per numbered step and descriptive link text.
3. Check keyboard access, text alternatives, contrast, and mobile readability.
4. Have a qualified reviewer confirm meaning before publishing translated content.

```text
Plain-language help template
Purpose: [what this task accomplishes]
Before you start: [approved prerequisites]
1. Open [page name].
2. Select [control name].
3. Review [items to verify].
4. Ask [authorized support] if anything is unclear.
Expected result: [verified result]

General Spanish help example for human review:
Revise la información antes de continuar. Si necesita ayuda, comuníquese con la oficina de la escuela.
```

## quality

### Prepare synthetic software quality checks

Use synthetic fixtures and maintained specifications. Verify tenant isolation, permissions, failure handling, accessibility, and human review. Do not upload production databases, credentials, private logs, or identifiable screenshots to an external service.

1. Define expected behavior and a synthetic fixture.
2. Test authorized and unauthorized roles and school boundaries.
3. Test unavailable services, malformed input, and cancellation.
4. Verify accessible layout, source labels, and review requirements.

```text
Synthetic test case outline
Feature and expected behavior
Synthetic role and school fixtures
Authorized success scenario
Unauthorized and cross-school rejection scenarios
Invalid-input and unavailable-service scenarios
Keyboard, mobile, and reduced-motion checks
Evidence and remaining limitations
```

| Accessibility/language | Plain-language template, general Spanish example requiring review | Qualified translation and accessibility validation of actual content |
| Software quality | Synthetic test planning checklist | Development-time tooling and independently verified tests |

External content-generation providers remain blocked. No search over arbitrary school content, translation
engine, demographic feed, synthetic-data generator, or automatic lesson generator
is claimed by this catalog. Follow [the existing privacy boundary](SOLOMON_PROVIDER_PRIVACY_BOUNDARY.md)
for provider contracts, legal/impact review, outbound controls, cost limits,
evaluations, and environment-specific release evidence. Keep calculations,
authorization, financial aid, grades, attendance, payments, and consequential
school decisions in their established rules-based and human workflows.

Validation: backend catalog/privacy/API tests; frontend selection, exact request,
plain-text draft and unsafe-source rejection tests; native browser smoke contract.
Tests use synthetic data. CI browser proof is not deployed-runtime certification.
