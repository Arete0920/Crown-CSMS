## Active-tree rule

Keep active source, tests, migrations, build and deployment controls, and current architecture, engineering, security, operations, provenance, and transfer documentation. Exclude generated proof dumps, copied conversations, marketing or transaction strategy, obsolete completion boards, retired automation, and superseded status files.

Normal onboarding must be tool-neutral and must not direct readers to editor-specific, assistant-specific, prompt-specific, agent-session, or retired proof material.
