# Arete Responsible Intelligence and Technology Governance — Christian School Assessment v1

**Status:** human-led advisory instrument. Not a compliance certificate, legal opinion, security audit, or authorization to enable CROWN functions. Requirements depend on a school's jurisdiction, funding, contracts, ages and actual data handling. Appropriate school and qualified legal/security authorities must assess applicability.

## Assessment method

Interview the head of school, instructional leader, school business officer, designated privacy/security lead and a board representative. Request policy/process **evidence references**, not copies of student records, confidential finance, raw survey answers, passwords or school credentials. For every control record an accountable owner, review date, limited-evidence description, risk, proposed action, deadline and approval authority.

Record **0** absent/unsafe; **1** informal/partially operating; **2** documented and implemented; **3** documented, tested and independently reviewed; **NE** not evaluated. Do not convert missing evidence to a zero or a pass. Do not calculate a composite school-health or spiritual-health score. Any unresolved critical child-safety, privacy, tenant authorization, data-integrity or human-decision problem means **NOT READY** regardless of other controls.

## School deliverables and approval

1. **Control register** with each RIT ID, evidence date, assessed state/NE, limitations, owner, and severity. These are control observations, not rankings of people or spiritual condition.
2. **Remediation plan** with priority, proposed action, assigned school decision owner, due/review date, scope and verification criteria.
3. **Go/no-go decision** identifying critical unresolved gaps, intended pilot or production scope, rollback approach, authorized human decision maker and next review. Missing permission or legal/data evidence is **NOT READY**, not inferred approval.

The Solomon [offline quality benchmark](../solomon/SOLOMON_QUALITY_EVALUATION_BENCHMARK.md) tests curated guidance integrity only; it does not certify a school or future generated output. CROWN Compass [#258](https://github.com/Arete0920/Crown-CSMS/issues/258) and Survey Intelligence [#204](https://github.com/Arete0920/Crown-CSMS/issues/204) retain independent certification and release gates. No student faith, character, salvation or spiritual-value scoring, autonomous consequential actions, external export of sensitive survey answers, fabricated live forecasts or bypass of existing security controls is permitted under this assessment.
