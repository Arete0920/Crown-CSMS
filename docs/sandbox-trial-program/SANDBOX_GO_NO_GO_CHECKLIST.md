## No-go conditions

Stop expansion if any of the following occur:

- repeated login failures,
- wrong school context shown,
- dashboard certification regression,
- wizard issue blocking most reviewers,
- support intake not functioning,
- unclear known-limitations communication,
- VIP cohort feedback indicates the product is not understandable without live support.

## Decision log

Every tier movement decision must record:

- date,
- tier,
- decision,
- evidence reviewed,
- unresolved issues,
- owner,
- next review date.
