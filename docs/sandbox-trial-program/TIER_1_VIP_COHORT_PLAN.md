# Tier 1 VIP Sandbox Cohort Plan

**Audience:** VIPs, buyers, partners, and high-value prospects
**Target size:** 15 schools or review teams
**Entry condition:** Tier 0 internal review completed with no open critical issue.

## Purpose

Tier 1 validates external usability and buyer confidence with a small, high-value cohort before opening CROWN to larger groups.

## Recommended cohort mix

| Segment | Target count | Reason |
|---------|--------------|--------|
| Prior soft-walkthrough schools | 8-10 | Already warm and able to compare progress |
| Potential buyers | 3-4 | Validate purchase interest and fit |
| Strategic partners | 1-2 | Validate partnership and implementation pathways |
| Trusted advisors | 1-2 | Provide candid product and market feedback |

## Selection criteria

Invite schools or partners that are:

- already interested in CROWN,
- constructive rather than purely critical,
- representative of the target Christian/private school market,
- able to give specific feedback,
- willing to treat the environment as a sandbox trial rather than production software.

## Tier 1 review objectives

1. Confirm the school-facing instructions are clear.
2. Validate login and onboarding flow.
3. Confirm dashboards communicate operational value.
4. Identify confusing workflow terminology.
5. Evaluate readiness for deeper buyer conversations.
6. Detect any issue that would block Tier 2 expansion.

## Suggested first-week rhythm

| Day | School activity | Internal activity |
|-----|-----------------|------------------|
| Day 1 | Login and navigation | Track access issues |
| Day 2 | Role-specific dashboard review | Triage dashboard feedback |
| Day 3 | Admissions, billing, registrar, gradebook, communications | Triage workflow feedback |
| Day 4 | Wizards, reports, and operational dashboards | Identify common blockers |
| Day 5 | Submit final feedback | Decide Tier 2 readiness |

## Tier 1 exit gate

Tier 2 may begin only when:

- No P0 issue remains open.
- No repeated login issue affects multiple schools.
- No tenant visibility issue is reported.
- Feedback has been categorized and assigned.
- Known limitations have been understood by the cohort.
- At least 70 percent of Tier 1 participants can complete the basic review path.

## Recommended communication posture

Use controlled language:

- CROWN is ready for sandbox evaluation.
- This is not a production launch.
- The goal is structured feedback and fit evaluation.
- School feedback will shape rollout priorities.
