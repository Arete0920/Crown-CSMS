## 1. Parties

This Data Processing Addendum is entered into between:

- **Customer / School:** `[Customer legal name]`
- **Provider:** `[CROWN legal entity / operator]`

## 2. Definitions

- **Customer Data:** Data submitted to, stored in, processed by, or generated through CROWN on behalf of Customer.
- **Student Data:** Customer Data relating to students, applicants, parents/guardians, households, academic records, attendance, behavior, health-office workflows, financial aid, tuition/billing, communications, and school operations.
- **Personal Information / Personal Data:** Any information that identifies or can reasonably be linked to an individual.
- **Services:** CROWN Christian School Management Solution and related support, hosting, implementation, and maintenance services.
- **Subprocessor:** Any third party engaged to process Customer Data on Provider's behalf.

## 3. Data ownership

Customer retains all right, title, and interest in Customer Data. Provider receives no ownership interest in Customer Data.

## 4. Processing instructions

Provider will process Customer Data only:

1. To provide the Services.
2. As configured by Customer-authorized users.
3. As documented in the agreement, order form, or this DPA.
4. As required by law, with notice to Customer unless legally prohibited.

Provider will not sell Customer Data, use Student Data for behavioral advertising, or use Customer Data for unrelated profiling.

## 5. FERPA / school official support

Where Customer determines that Provider acts as a school official or service provider for FERPA-covered education records, Provider will:

- Use education records only for legitimate educational interests and contracted Services.
- Maintain confidentiality of education records.
- Refrain from redisclosing education records except as authorized by Customer, this DPA, or law.
- Support Customer's response to parent/eligible-student access, amendment, and disclosure-record requests as applicable.

## 6. COPPA support

Where Services are used by children under 13 under school direction, Customer is responsible for determining the lawful consent model. Provider will process such data only for school-authorized educational or operational purposes and will not use it for behavioral advertising or unrelated commercial profiling.

## 7. Confidentiality

Provider personnel with access to Customer Data must be bound by confidentiality obligations and access Customer Data only as necessary for authorized purposes.

## 8. Security controls

Provider will maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of Customer Data, including:

- Tenant isolation.
- Role-based access control.
- Least-privilege administrative access.
- Encryption in transit.
- Encryption at rest where supported by hosting/storage providers.
- Audit logging for privileged access and data exports.
- Secure SDLC practices.
- Secret-management controls.
- Vulnerability remediation process.
- Backup and restore process.

## 9. Support access

Provider support access to Customer Data must be approved, limited to necessary scope, time-bounded where technically feasible, and logged.

Emergency access must be documented and reviewed after the event.

## 10. Incident notice

Provider will notify Customer without undue delay after confirming a security incident involving Customer Data. Notice should include, to the extent known:

- Nature of incident.
- Data categories affected.
- Approximate number of affected records/users/tenants.
- Mitigation actions.
- Corrective actions.
- Contact for follow-up.

Cross-tenant exposure is treated as critical unless disproven.

## 11. Subprocessors

Provider may use subprocessors only for Services delivery. Provider will maintain a subprocessor register identifying vendor, purpose, data categories, and contract/security status.

Provider remains responsible for subprocessors' processing of Customer Data to the extent required by the agreement.

## 12. Data retention, return, and deletion

Upon termination or Customer request, Provider will return, delete, or de-identify Customer Data according to the agreement, applicable law, technical feasibility, backup-retention rules, and Customer instructions.

Provider may retain audit logs or records where required for legal, compliance, financial, security, or dispute-resolution purposes, subject to confidentiality and access controls.

## 13. Backups

Backups will be retained only for documented restore windows. Backup restore operations must follow tenant-aware procedures and be restricted to authorized personnel.

## 14. Audit and cooperation

Provider will reasonably cooperate with Customer's compliance obligations by providing relevant documentation, security summaries, subprocessors, incident reports, and evidence of controls, subject to confidentiality and security restrictions.

## 15. Data location

Data hosting location/region must be documented in the customer order form or deployment specification where applicable.

## 16. Order of precedence

If this DPA conflicts with the main agreement, the controlling order must be defined by the executed contract.
