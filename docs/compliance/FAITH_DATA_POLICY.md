# FAITH DATA POLICY

## Purpose

CROWN processes faith-related educational data solely for
school-directed educational, discipleship, pastoral, and mission purposes.

## Covered Sensitive Faith Data

- Prayer requests
- Spiritual life tracking
- Discipleship participation
- Church affiliation
- Statement of faith records
- Mission/service participation
- Pastoral intervention notes

## Prohibited Uses

CROWN SHALL NOT:

- sell faith data
- profile students commercially
- use faith data for advertising
- perform cross-tenant analytics on faith data
- disclose faith data to unauthorized third parties

## Access Controls

Faith data access is restricted using:

- tenant isolation
- RBAC permissions
- audit logging
- least-privilege enforcement

## Export Restrictions

Faith data exports require explicit authorization and
must not be included automatically in generalized exports.
