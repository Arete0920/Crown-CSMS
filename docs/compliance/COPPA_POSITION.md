# CROWN COPPA Position

**Status:** Documented product/compliance position — not legal certification.

## Product position

Where COPPA applies, CROWN is intended to operate only for school-authorized educational and school-operational purposes unless a separate lawful parental-consent path is implemented and approved.

CROWN must not use school authorization to justify behavioral advertising, sale of children's personal information, unrelated commercial profiling, unrelated model training or product enrichment, or collection materially beyond the authorized service purpose.

## Required controls

For under-13 experiences, CROWN requires:

1. clear notice of collection, use, and disclosure practices;
2. age- and role-appropriate account controls;
3. school/tenant binding for school-authorized child accounts;
4. collection limited to the minimum information needed for the authorized purpose;
5. reasonable confidentiality, security, and integrity safeguards;
6. school/parent review and deletion support where applicable;
7. approved retention and deletion behavior;
8. no behavioral advertising or sale of child/student data;
9. an explicit consent path for any direct-to-child use that cannot rely on school authorization.

Direct child signup must fail closed unless the approved consent model for that use is present.

## AI and automated-processing boundary

Any future external generative-AI capability must follow CROWN privacy, purpose-limitation, and data-minimization controls. Personally identifiable child/student information must not be transmitted to an external generative-AI service merely to provide convenience features. Student-facing generative AI is not authorized by this policy by default.

Any AI-specific deployment requires separate privacy/security review, processor terms, retention/data-use verification, and a documented purpose.

## School responsibility

The school remains responsible for determining whether school authorization is legally available for its use, providing required notices, and obtaining parental consent where required. CROWN must provide the information and controls needed to support that decision.

## Claim boundary

CROWN must not be described as "COPPA certified" or universally COPPA-compliant. Legal applicability depends on the service, users, data flow, consent path, jurisdiction, contracts, and actual deployed operation.
