# CROWN Intellectual Property Design Origin Declaration

**Document Class:** Legal / Intellectual Property
**Organization:** Crown Christian School Management Solutions
**Prepared by:** Engineering Leadership
**Repository:** Arete0920/Crown-CSMS

## I. Original design statement

CROWN source, architecture, workflows, domain models, and product-specific terminology are intended to be independently designed.

The repository must not incorporate proprietary source code, private implementation artifacts, non-public schemas, or copied commercial product materials.

No contributor is authorized to reverse engineer commercial software for replication.

## II. Architecture origin

CROWN architecture is derived from:

- standard web-application and multi-tenant engineering practices;
- direct K-12 operational requirements;
- accounting and ledger principles;
- data-governance and security principles;
- CROWN's canonical domain model;
- Christian-school mission and operational requirements;
- documented product requirements and architecture decisions.

Examples of independently governed CROWN areas include:

- tenant and school scoping;
- role and permission enforcement;
- student/household/staff identity;
- enrollment lifecycle;
- academics and gradebook;
- finance and ledger;
- financial-aid workflow;
- scheduling;
- communications;
- spiritual-life and mission workflows;
- implementation/release controls.

## III. Market-awareness boundary

General market awareness may identify broad categories of school-management capability.

External commercial products must not be used as:

- design specifications;
- implementation templates;
- functional blueprints;
- schema references;
- UI replication sources.

Industry-standard concepts such as enrollment, attendance, billing, scheduling, gradebook, reporting, and ledger accounting are implemented through CROWN's own architecture and business rules.

## IV. Repository IP hygiene

Repository policy requires periodic scans for:

- named competitors;
- imitation language;
- copied proprietary terminology;
- unsupported third-party attribution;
- suspicious external implementation references.

Current repository content should use neutral terms such as "legacy source system" or "external school-management platform" when third-party systems must be discussed operationally.

## V. Third-party dependencies

Third-party libraries must be used under their applicable licenses and tracked through supported dependency-management files.

Dependency use does not authorize reuse of proprietary commercial software.

## VI. Branding independence

CROWN-specific product names and workflow terminology are governed by CROWN's own branding and legal-review process.

The repository does not make comparative trademark assertions about named external products.

## VII. Declaration

To the best of the owner's knowledge:

- CROWN is intended as an independently designed work;
- proprietary competitor code is not authorized for use;
- reverse engineering for replication is prohibited;
- repository content should remain free of named competitor references;
- external market awareness is limited to general problem-domain understanding.

This declaration records repository governance intent and should be reviewed with qualified counsel for transaction or litigation purposes.
