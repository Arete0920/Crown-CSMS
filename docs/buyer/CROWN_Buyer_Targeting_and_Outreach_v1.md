# CROWN Buyer Targeting and Outreach Plan

## Confidentiality status

**Internal controlled material.** Potential acquirers, investors, sponsors, channel partners, pilot partners, and integration partners must be referenced by role or category only. No prospective organization may be named in buyer-facing, diligence-facing, client-facing, future-owner-facing, repository-status, presentation, handout, or outreach materials unless the Founder/Product Owner later gives explicit authorization.

## Objective

Identify and approach organizations capable of acquiring, funding, distributing, integrating, or accelerating CROWN while preserving its Christian-school market focus and supporting a 24-month founder-led transition.

## Targeting principles

A qualified prospect should demonstrate several of the following:

- clear alignment with Christian education, faith-and-flourishing, or mission-driven technology;
- ability to fund and operate a software acquisition;
- established distribution to schools, churches, ministries, or Christian families;
- product, data, implementation, payments, or customer-success capabilities;
- willingness to preserve CROWN's Christian-school identity and mission fit;
- capacity to support production hardening and commercial scale.

Prospects must be classified by likely role rather than published as a named list.

## Priority prospect categories

### Category 1 — Strategic platform acquirer

**Likely role:** Acquirer or platform sponsor capable of operating a faith-centered technology ecosystem.

**Primary discussion:** CROWN's product breadth, Christian-school specialization, data and engagement potential, current controlled-release position, and founder-led 24-month transfer.

**Evidence to prepare:**

- architecture and platform map;
- release-readiness summary;
- school-market problem statement;
- commercial model with assumptions clearly labeled;
- integration and ecosystem thesis;
- intellectual-property and transition summary.

### Category 2 — Mission-aligned capital or strategic sponsor

**Likely role:** Investor, sponsor, commercialization partner, or joint-venture participant aligned with Christian education.

**Primary discussion:** CROWN as enabling infrastructure for stronger school operations, governance, finance, leadership, and mission—not merely as a software asset.

**Evidence to prepare:**

- mission-impact framework;
- school operating outcomes;
- pilot model;
- governance and measurement plan;
- capital requirements and use of funds;
- acquisition, investment, and partnership alternatives.

### Category 3 — Distribution and market-validation partner

**Likely role:** Channel, validation, pilot, content, professional-development, or member-benefit partner with direct Christian-school reach.

**Primary discussion:** Controlled pilots, member needs, conference visibility, research validation, professional-development alignment, and nonexclusive partnership structures.

### Category 4 — Network and pilot partner

**Likely role:** Christian-school network, international pilot, market-validation, or mission-impact partner.

**Primary discussion:** Strategic learning and controlled validation across varied school operating environments rather than an assumed acquisition process.

### Category 5 — Commercial integration partner

**Likely role:** Payments, billing, financial-services, identity, communications, or operational integration partner; acquisition potential only after separate diligence.

**Constraint:** No acquisition, exclusivity, provider activation, or revenue-sharing claim may be made without a written agreement and applicable legal, compliance, security, underwriting, and operational review.

## Outreach sequence

### Stage 1 — Confidential preparation

- finalize acquisition brief and presentation;
- complete buyer-facing release disclosure;
- prepare transaction framework and diligence index;
- confirm intellectual-property and contributor records;
- separate verified facts from modeled assumptions;
- keep prospect identities outside broadly distributed materials.

### Stage 2 — Controlled introduction strategy

Use trusted relationships, conferences, advisors, board members, ministry leaders, investors, and sector contacts to seek confidential introductions to authorized senior decision makers.

The first communication should request a confidential strategic discussion. It should not publish a prospect list, distribute the entire data room, or lead with unsupported valuation or interest claims.

### Stage 3 — Initial meeting

Cover:

- strategic problem and product thesis;
- why CROWN may fit the prospect category;
- current product and release status;
- acquisition and 24-month transition concept;
- commercial assumptions and validation needs;
- decision process and next diligence step.

### Stage 4 — Controlled diligence

After appropriate confidentiality protections are in place, provide staged access to:

- product and architecture materials;
- repository and release evidence;
- security, privacy, and operational evidence;
- commercial model and pipeline evidence;
- intellectual-property and contributor documentation;
- transaction and transition framework.

## Approved outreach language

CROWN is a unified Christian school management platform developed to address fragmented student, financial, operational, communications, governance, and mission-centered systems. We are evaluating confidential strategic acquisition, sponsorship, investment, channel, and integration relationships that may include the CROWN platform and intellectual property together with a 24-month founder-led transition. We believe there may be strong alignment with selected organizations serving Christian education and faith-centered technology, and we welcome a confidential introductory discussion.

## Qualification questions

- Does the organization have authority and capacity to acquire, sponsor, fund, distribute, or integrate a software platform?
- Is Christian-school technology part of its strategic agenda?
- What existing product, payments, data, association, or school relationships affect fit?
- Would it preserve CROWN's Christian-school positioning?
- Can it support product operations, implementation, customer success, security, and compliance?
- What evidence and milestones are required before an indication of interest?
- Who controls the decision and diligence process?

## Current recommendation

Use a role-based process and maintain the named prospect roster separately under restricted access. Public or broadly shared materials may state that CROWN has identified potential strategic buyers and partners, but must not identify them by name or imply that any prospect has expressed interest without direct evidence.
