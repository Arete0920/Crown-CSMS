## Common pilot response conventions

- Audience is exclusively adults: no students or minors, no direct invitations to children.
- Participation is optional and must not change admission, re-enrollment or access to school services.
- Default question type: one response per statement on an ordinal agreement scale: 1 Strongly disagree; 2 Disagree; 3 Neither agree nor disagree; 4 Agree; 5 Strongly agree.
- Mark these scale statements **optional** until a neutral not-applicable/skip control is verified; do not force answers that do not apply.
- The scale is ordinal; show distributions, response counts and question-level nonresponse, not a validated psychological score or causal conclusion.
- Do not ask respondent name, household, student, grade, precise demographic subgroup, income, faith affiliation or personal circumstances in an anonymous instrument.
- Do not attach respondent ID, individual campaign or application reference, analytics fingerprint or freeform metadata to answers.
- No open text in the initial anonymous pilot: free-text re-identification controls are not yet production certified.
- For all instruments, ask one section at a time and provide a clear voluntary skip and exit path.
- Keep questions identical within a published version and retain scale anchors with that version.

## CP-PE-01 — Parent Experience

**Purpose:** Identify school communication and family-service opportunities, not assess student spirituality or individual staff performance. **Survey owner:** Head of school or delegated school leader. **Recommended reference window:** Current academic term.

| Key | Independently authored adult-facing prompt | Type | Required | Analysis domain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pe_updates | The school's regular updates help me understand what families need to know. | scale | No | Communication |
| pe_access | I can find the school information I need without unnecessary effort. | scale | No | Family service |
| pe_timeliness | Questions I submit through the school's usual channels receive a timely response. | scale | No | Responsiveness |
| pe_expectations | School expectations for family participation are explained clearly. | scale | No | Family partnership |
| pe_instruction | The school communicates its academic priorities in understandable terms. | scale | No | Academic communication |
| pe_mission | School communications explain how planned activities connect to the school's stated mission. | scale | No | Mission communication |
| pe_value | The school explains the programs and services included in its published fees. | scale | No | Transparency and perceived value |
| pe_change | I know how to suggest an improvement and how the school follows up. | scale | No | Improvement transparency |

**Interpretation limits:** Results describe respondents' reported experiences during the window. Do not label individual families at risk, infer board or faculty effectiveness, or predict retention from these answers alone.

## CP-PT-01 — Post-Tour Feedback

**Purpose:** Improve clarity, access and follow-up in school tours, without recording identities or private family deliberations. **Owner:** Admissions leadership. **Reference window:** Most recent tour.

| Key | Independently authored adult-facing prompt | Type | Required | Analysis domain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| pt_welcome | The visit helped me feel oriented to the campus and its daily activities. | scale | No | Tour experience |
| pt_program | I received a clear explanation of the school's learning program. | scale | No | Academic clarity |
| pt_values | I understood how the school describes its mission and educational values. | scale | No | Mission explanation |
| pt_practical | The visit clarified the practical steps involved in applying. | scale | No | Admissions process |
| pt_cost | I learned where to find accurate tuition, fee and assistance information. | scale | No | Affordability information |
| pt_questions | I had a reasonable opportunity to ask questions. | scale | No | Responsiveness |
| pt_nextsteps | I know what to do if I want additional information or another visit. | scale | No | Next-step clarity |

**Interpretation limits:** An anonymous survey cannot reliably determine which inquiry converted, or calculate prospect-specific outcomes; compare only permitted aggregate periods and verified operational funnels.

## CP-RI-01 — Re-enrollment Intent and Information Needs

**Purpose:** Understand aggregate information barriers and broad intention, not forecast any family's actual enrollment. **Owner:** Enrollment leadership. **Reference window:** Upcoming re-enrollment decision period.

| Key | Independently authored adult-facing prompt | Type | Required | Analysis domain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ri_plan | I currently expect to consider re-enrollment at this school for the next school year. | scale | No | Stated intention |
| ri_dates | I understand when the school's re-enrollment steps must be completed. | scale | No | Process clarity |
| ri_costinfo | I know where to find current tuition and fee information for the coming year. | scale | No | Financial transparency |
| ri_aidinfo | I know where to ask about available tuition assistance and its application process. | scale | No | Assistance information |
| ri_program | The school has explained its academic and program priorities for next year. | scale | No | Program understanding |
| ri_contact | I know whom to contact for a confidential question about re-enrollment. | scale | No | School support |
| ri_fairness | The school's re-enrollment communications explain decisions and deadlines consistently. | scale | No | Process confidence |

**Interpretation limits:** An intent statement does not establish actual re-enrollment, a validated risk score, affordability status or a causal relationship. Only actual canonical admissions/enrollment records can substantiate enrollment outcomes.
