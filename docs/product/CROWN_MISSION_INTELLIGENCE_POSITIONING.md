# Crown Mission Intelligence Positioning

## Positioning Thesis
Crown does not compete by offering generic black-box automated decisioning.

Crown competes by offering mission-aligned, governance-safe, transparent institutional intelligence for Christian schools.

## Official Position
Crown Compass is Crown’s mission-aligned governance and institutional strategy platform.

It is powered by the Discernment Engine, which provides explainable predictive analytics and scenario modeling within institution-defined constraints.

Crown Solomon provides the mission-aware knowledge, governance templates, interpretation guidance, and action playbooks that help leadership use Compass responsibly.

## Why This Matters
Christian school boards and leaders often resist opaque algorithmic systems.

Crown’s advantage is not algorithmic mystique.
Crown’s advantage is:
- transparency
- auditability
- mission-defined constraints
- human accountability
- board-ready outputs
- mission-aware implementation support

## Approved Public Messaging
- transparent predictive intelligence
- mission-aligned strategic forecasting
- governance-safe scenario modeling
- auditable institutional insights
- leadership-defined parameters
- board-ready explainable outputs

## Messaging to Avoid
- black-box automated decisioning
- autonomous decisions
- algorithm says
- auto-recommendation engine
- machine-guided discipline
- spiritual score
- character score

## Summary Statement
Crown helps Christian school leaders see more clearly, govern more responsibly, and act more faithfully through transparent, explainable institutional intelligence.
