# Public language policy

Owner decision: 2026-10-02.

Product labels, dashboards, marketing, and current user guidance describe approved capabilities and staff workflows. Use plain operational terms such as editorial review, statistical prediction, forecasting, and decision support when those terms accurately describe the implementation.

Do not introduce authoring-tool names, provider identities, implementation-method brands, or automated-drafting branding into those surfaces. Rephrase existing wording to describe the actual capability and responsible human role. Unimplemented proposals must not appear as available features.

Renaming must preserve meaning. A wording change does not remove an algorithm, integration, obligation, or risk. Maintain truthful internal method records, data contracts, and review requirements. Do not claim a process is absent based only on a label change.

Preserve applied migration identifiers, required third-party license/copyright attribution, and authentic evidence. Do not falsify historical inventories or strip signed provenance from retained media. Record residual references as explicit audit exceptions.

Owner extension: 2026-10-12. Obsolete generated inventories may be retired from the current tree as whole records, with their original commits and retirement reasons recorded. Signed media may be replaced by newly authored assets; retain the original in version history rather than altering its authenticity record. Optional package funding links may be removed without changing licenses, integrity hashes, versions, or dependency relationships. Reinstalls may restore upstream funding metadata; it is not an authoring-method claim.

Review current content and user-facing labels for policy compliance before merging. Keep operational approvals, publication review, source attribution, and release limitations intact.

## Pull-request and contribution narrative

Pull-request titles and authored descriptions are maintained first-party language and must follow this policy. Describe the functional change, review evidence, remaining risks, human accountability, and rollback without promotional method labels or unsupported attribution. A required repository check reviews this narrative at new and edited pull requests.

This rule does not authorize rewriting immutable commit metadata, original contribution records, historical discussions, signed media, upstream license notices, or security disclosures. Preserve true contributors and essential integration identifiers in appropriate technical evidence. Any conflict between neutral public language and truthful technical disclosure requires an explicit, narrowly scoped owner-reviewed disposition; never conceal an active dependency or fabricate an author.
